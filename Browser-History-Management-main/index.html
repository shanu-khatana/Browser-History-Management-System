      About Browser History Manager

      Browser History Manager is a powerful tool designed to help you take control of your browsing history. With our intuitive interface, you can easily track, search, and navigate through your visited pages.

      Key Features

      • Track all your visited URLs with visit counts
      • Search through your history quickly and efficiently
      • Navigate back and forward through your browsing history
      • Bookmark your favorite sites for quick access
      • Sort and filter your history by various criteria
      • Secure user authentication and personalized data storage

      How It Works

      Our system stores your browsing history securely and allows you to manage it with ease. Simply login, visit URLs through our interface, and let us handle the rest. Your data is kept separate and secure for each user account.

      Note: This is a demonstration project showcasing history management capabilities. All data is stored locally in your browser.
